First Stage FABLES
Timeline
Race / Species
Tiefling
Class
Warlock
Background
Noble, turned Failed Doctor
Alignment
Lawful Evil (definite code of right & wrong, the law, clear understanding of own beliefs and will follow that to the extent of going against morality. For the Greater Good)

Character Concept

Failed doctor (haematologist) turned blood mage who after his experiments were discovered back in the Crownlands, volunteered himself for an expedition into the unknown. [note 1]

Three Words That Describe Them:

  1. Ambitious
  2. Pragmatic
  3. Cutthroat

What do they look like?

Ashen-skinned Tiefling. Pale, sickly almost in appearance. Still dresses like a nobleman. Waistcoat, long dinner jacket, silver-clawed gauntlet, medical reference books tucked into his pack. Covered in small packs and little leather pouches. Dinner coat full of pockets.

Hair: Long hair down his back. Black in color. Partial braid. Also still carries around medical equipment, (((syringes))).

Shorter horns.

Thinks he can heal. Knows he can harm.
